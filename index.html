Hello! I am an Undergraduate Researcher in the Speech & Interactive AI Laboratory (SIA) at University of Seoul (UOS), advised by Prof. Heeseung Kim. I am also a senior undergraduate student in the Department of GeoInformatics & AI at UOS. Previously, I worked as an Undergraduate Research Intern in the Statistical Inference and Information Theory Lab (SIIT) at KAIST (advised by Prof. Junmo Kim) and the Robotics and Computer Vision Lab (RCV) at UOS (advised by Prof. Jiyoung Jung).

I am broadly interested in Trustworthy Computer Vision, aiming to ensure models operate reliably and safely in open, real-world environments. My research has primarily centered on 3D point cloud Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) and adversarial defense, enabling models to dynamically adapt to severe distribution shifts and corruptions. Recently, I have expanded my focus toward omni-modal foundation models, with a particular emphasis on hallucination mitigation and calibration for robust real-world multimodal applications.
