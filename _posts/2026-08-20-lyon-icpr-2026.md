## Parc de la Tête d'Or

Mornings at the large park in the north of the city: a still lake under the trees, geese on the shore, and antelopes in the zoo inside the park.

## City streets

Pale yellow buildings in the 6th arrondissement, at midday and at sunset.

## Presqu'île and the Saône

The fountain at Place des Jacobins, and the view across the Saône to the Fourvière hill, with the basilica on top and Saint-Jean Cathedral below.
