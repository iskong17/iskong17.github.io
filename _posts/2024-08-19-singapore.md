I visited Singapore from August 16 to 19, 2024, about a month before the Singapore Grand Prix. The city was already getting ready for the race, and much of my time was spent around Marina Bay.

## Above the circuit: the Singapore Flyer

The view from the **Singapore Flyer** looks straight down onto the pit straight of the [Marina Bay Street Circuit](/blog/race/circuits/marina_bay/). The pit building, the paddock and the main grandstand, with "SINGAPORE" spelled out in its seats, were already in place, with the Benjamin Sheares Bridge and the National Stadium behind them. For an F1 fan, it is a rare chance to see the track from above a few weeks before the race.

![The Marina Bay Street Circuit pit straight and grandstand seen from the Singapore Flyer](/images/blog/singapore-2024/flyer-view-marina-bay-circuit.jpg)

## Flowers on film

I also brought a film camera. The date stamp in the corner reads 16 August 2024, the first day of the trip. Singapore's greenery is everywhere, even between buildings, like this patch of red and pink geraniums.

![Red and pink geraniums among green leaves, shot on film](/images/blog/singapore-2024/film-flowers.jpg)

## North Bridge Road

Walking along **North Bridge Road**, the skyline mixes very different styles. On the left is the Art Deco Parkview Square, and next to it the hexagon-patterned glass towers of DUO, with gardens planted on their terraces.

![Parkview Square and the DUO towers from North Bridge Road](/images/blog/singapore-2024/north-bridge-road.jpg)

## Marina Bay Sands

From directly below, **Marina Bay Sands** is hard to fit in one frame. The three hotel towers lean toward each other, and the SkyPark sits across the top like a ship, with plants growing along every floor of the facade.

![Marina Bay Sands from directly below](/images/blog/singapore-2024/marina-bay-sands.jpg)
